Selected work · 2024 – 2026

Products I've shipped

Three projects with the outcome first. I work end to end: discovery, design in the browser, build, launch, and the measuring after.

Sanduku

Fintech · marketing site + onboarding · 2026 · Lead front-end

A savings app for chamas (group savings circles) needed sign-ups to finish on cheap Android phones over patchy data. I rebuilt the onboarding as a six-step flow that saves progress locally and resumes after a dropped connection.

Outcome
Sign-up completion 41% → 68% in the first month
Built
Next.js, WebGL hero, Stripe + M-Pesa hand-off, Playwright suite
Weight
First load 92 KB on 3G, Lighthouse 99

Baraza Health

Telehealth · patient portal + design system · 2025 · Full-stack

Clinics were booking over WhatsApp and losing appointments. I built a patient portal with booking, reminders, and a 42-component design system the in-house team now ships with.

Outcome
No-shows down 31%, 4 clinics onboarded without me
Built
React, Node, Postgres, Africa's Talking SMS, Storybook
Access
WCAG 2.1 AA audited, keyboard-complete

Kilele Labs

Climate data · interactive report + dashboard · 2024 · Front-end + dataviz

A research lab's annual report was a 90-page PDF nobody read. I turned it into a scroll-driven story with live charts and a dashboard that updates from their data pipeline.

Outcome
Median time on report 0:40 → 6:10, cited by two ministries
Built
SvelteKit, D3, Canvas charts, static export on Cloudflare
Scale
1.2M data points rendered under 16 ms per frame

About · the story so far

Hi, I'm Michael

I'm a web developer in Nairobi. I got into this by fixing a hardware shop's website in 2016 and never really stopped. I like products that feel good in the hand, fast, tactile, honest about what they do, and I care about them working on the phones people actually own.

  1. 2026

    Independent, product-focused

    Working with two or three founders at a time on launches. Started the WebGL and motion work that this island and its sibling designs came out of.

  2. 2024 – 2025

    Front-end lead, Baraza Health (contract)

    Design system, patient portal, accessibility audit. Grew the front-end team from one to three.

  3. 2022 – 2024

    Full-stack developer, agency in Nairobi

    Shipped 14 sites and two web apps for clients across East Africa. Learned to estimate honestly and to say no to a fourth carousel.

  4. 2019 – 2022

    BSc Computer Science + freelance

    Built for local businesses while studying. First paid WebGL job was a 3D product spinner for a furniture maker.

  5. 2016

    The hardware shop website

    My uncle's shop. Table-based layout, one broken image, and the moment I decided this was the job.

How I work

  • Design in the browser. Real sizes, real content, real devices, from week one.
  • A frame budget on day one. 16 ms on a mid-range Android, measured, not assumed.
  • Small releases. Weekly, behind flags when it's risky, with a number attached to each one.
  • Write it down. Decisions, trade-offs and the "why" live in the repo, not in my head.

Right now

  • Learning Rust well enough to ship a WASM image tool
  • Reading Refactoring UI for the third time
  • Training for the Nairobi half marathon (slowly)
